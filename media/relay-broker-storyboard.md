# Relay broker concept film

42 seconds, 1280×720, 24 fps. English on-screen captions; intentionally silent.

1. Demand-led procurement.
2. Define resource, context and budget.
3. Reject wrong coverage; compare eligible quotes (fictional suppliers).
4. Automate selection, authorization, purchase and return.
5. Proposed USD customer experience; supplier-compatible settlement.
6. Result and receipt; current Testnet boundary.
7. Platform promise and first use case.

All quote comparisons are illustrative. USD collection, currency conversion and multi-rail routing are planned, not current capabilities. Ledger speed/fees are not end-to-end cost or latency measurements. No voiceover or music.

Sources: existing demo implementation and https://xrpl.org/about/xrp for ledger payment properties.
