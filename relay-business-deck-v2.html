Customer / Reason to pay

Lenders pay Relay to handle procurement

06 / 10

The paying customer is the lending institution.

The user is a loan officer or an underwriting system requesting supplemental information.

Relay aims to reduce separate supplier integrations and the work of sourcing, paying for and documenting occasional purchases.

Initial target hypothesis: lenders with recurring, low-volume needs across several data categories.

Customer segment and willingness to pay are hypotheses. A pilot should measure procurement effort, usable-result rate and repeat usage.
