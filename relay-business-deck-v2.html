One Relay integration. Your application keeps the business decision.

Platform vision: supplier selection and automated small purchases. Initial demo: underwriting data APIs. General resource support and customer billing remain planned.

First use case / Personal underwriting

First use case: personal underwriting

04 / 11

A loan officer is reviewing an individual’s loan application. The applicant works in the legal industry, and the officer needs context on changes that could affect their income.

The immediate task:
Request the missing income or industry context for this person.

If the applicant is a company owner flagged by the lender for further review, Relay may also be asked for the linked company’s registration record.

An application of the platform, not its product boundary. Company registration is a conditional supplemental check, not the default request. The lender identifies the information need; current mirrors return samples, not person-specific or legal-industry-specific research.

94.2% before network, FX, compliance and operating costs. This spread funds Relay’s service; it is not net profit.

Costs beyond the supplier

Fund the team and each purchase

Monthly fixed costs: 1 engineer + 1 sales hire, plus base hosting and operations.

Variable costs: payment / FX fees, slippage, validation, compute, support and failed purchases.

Operating result = paid queries × (US$0.4712 − other variable cost/query) − monthly fixed costs − trial costs.

Trial proposal: US$10 / 20 queries. Trial fulfilment costs also need funding.

US$0.50 is a data-use-case proposal, not a universal platform price. FX illustration: 1 XRP = 1.44 USDT; 1 USDT = US$1.00. Salaries and operating costs are not yet estimated. Fast conversion reduces exposure time, not fees or slippage. Current demo: XRP payment and internal cost + 17.5% ledger; USD collection / USDT conversion planned.

01 · QUOTE

Vendor → Relay

HTTP 402 specifies the amount, destination and network.

02 · AUTHORIZE

Relay → Vendor

Relay’s wallet signs the approved XRP Payment and retries with payment proof.

Facilitator → XRPL

The facilitator verifies and submits the payment for ledger validation.

04 · DELIVER

Vendor → Relay → App

Data and receipt return. Relay checks the transaction independently through XRPL RPC.

Open assumptions / What must hold

Working assumptions to challenge

11 / 11

The proxy creates value if it makes supplier selection and small purchases easier, reliable and economical.

TopicWorking positionWhat remains to prove
Customer value
Demo assumption		Applications need recurring small purchases; Relay can reduce repeated procurement work.Test preference for Relay over direct integrations. Underwriting is the first scenario, not the whole market.
Supplier access
Market hypothesis		Suppliers expose purchasable resources and sufficient metadata for selection.Verify coverage, usage rights and accepted payments. API availability does not justify putting data on-chain.
Data quality
Evidence requirement		The proxy can check delivery and contract fit; content assurance needs separate evidence.Define checks per resource; factual claims require source verification or independent cross-reference.
Agent reliability
Engineering work		Build contract checks, policy gates, duplicate-payment protection and reconciliation.Test mismatches, retries and failures. Engineering checks reduce errors; they do not guarantee none.
Payment experience
Routing hypothesis		Customers see USD. XRPL can provide fast, low-fee settlement behind the scenes.Confirm each vendor’s currency and network: USDT, XRP or USD. Measure complete cost and delivery time.
Economics & FX
Cost model		Rapid XRP conversion limits exposure time; spread must fund engineering, sales and operations.Include exchange fees, slippage, validation and failures. USDT/USD parity and conversion liquidity are assumptions.

Proven today: XRP Testnet payment and sample delivery. Still to prove: general supplier matching, content checks, customer billing and multi-rail payment support.

Demo assumptions and implementation work, not production-readiness claims. Vendor type does not establish currency or network acceptance. XRP is the asset; XRPL is the ledger. Detailed assumptions and data-contract design ↗
