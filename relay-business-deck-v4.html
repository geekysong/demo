Relay / Proposed workflow

Institution payment through its linked wallet

05 / 08
Financial institution → A site / app → linked institutional wallet or billing account
01 · BIND

Connect once

Bind the institution and A site to an approved payer account and spending limits.

02 · REQUEST

Carry payment context

Each query includes the site identity and wallet reference. Relay returns a quote.

03 · PAY

Authorize automatically

Pay within the institution’s standing mandate. Over-limit requests require approval.

04 · CONFIRM

Credit and reconcile

Confirm receipt once, credit the institution’s sub-wallet and reserve the query budget.

Fiat

Bank / payment provider → Relay collection account

USDT / stablecoins

Approved token and network → Relay receiving wallet

XBR · to confirm

Requested asset; ticker and network pending confirmation

The app carries a wallet reference and payment authorization; private signing keys stay with the wallet provider. Customer payment and supplier settlement may use different rails.

Proposed collection design, not a live payment integration. Automatic payment requires a prior mandate or signed authorization. Quotes specify asset, network, conversion rate, fees and expiry. Existing demo settles supplier payments in XRP on XRPL Testnet; XBR is not yet mapped to an asset.

Relay / Proposed workflow

Collection, sub-wallet allocation and settlement

06 / 08
Confirmed fiat / token receipt → Relay collection layer → institution + A site sub-wallet

Reserve the quoted amount for this query

Split by purpose after the request outcome is confirmed

01 / SUPPLIER

Supplier sub-wallets

Allocate each supplier’s payable amount. Settle on that supplier’s accepted rail.

02 / RELAY

Service fee sub-wallet

Allocate the disclosed Relay fee for the completed service.

03 / COSTS

Payment & FX sub-wallet

Allocate quoted processing, network and conversion costs.

04 / CUSTOMER

Remaining balance

Release unused reserves to the institution. Track eligible refunds to the original payer.

One reconciliation record: institution, site, query, incoming payment, allocations and outgoing settlements. No marketplace purchase on a consent-only query → no marketplace supplier allocation; any access and service fees remain explicit.

Proposed allocation model: sub-wallets represent segregated accounting balances; dedicated on-chain addresses and custody arrangements remain to be designed. Never treat client balances as earned revenue. Confirmed receipts = allocated costs / fees + supplier amounts + remaining / refundable balance, reconciled per asset.

Relay / Collection, payment & cost assumptions

Economics per completed query

07 / 08

01 / Customer funds Relay

Reserve the query price

Receive fiat or supported tokens into the institution balance. Reserve the quoted amount.

02 / Relay pays suppliers

Pay for each paid attempt

Pay the supplier’s quote on its accepted rail. Purchased data may fail Relay’s quality checks.

03 / Relay completes the query

Charge once, on completion

Deliver qualifying data and charge US$0.50. If incomplete, release or refund the reserve. Relay bears sunk costs.
