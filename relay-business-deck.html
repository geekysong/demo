Why Relay uses an agent and x402

Three costs never appear on the price sheet

03 / 07

Minimum-commitment tax

Occasional use converts a low unit price into a much higher effective cost.

Vendor integration surface

Each infrequent category can trigger its own contract, API integration, maintenance path, and vendor review.

Volume discount cliff

A small or mid-sized lender will not reach the supplied U.S.-analogue scale of ~10,000+ MAU through occasional supplemental checks.

“No API integration” has a precise meaning here: the lender avoids a separate contract-and-integration cycle for each infrequent vendor category. Relay still requires one integration into the lender's underwriting workflow.
